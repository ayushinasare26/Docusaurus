# Product List

The Product List section allows users to view and manage the products available in the Hexa Billing System.

![Product List](/img/product-list.png)

*Figure: Product List displaying product IDs, names, descriptions, monthly rent, product types, invoice status, and available actions.*

## Product Information

The Product List displays important information about each product, including:

- Product ID
- Product Name
- Description
- Monthly Rent
- Product Type
- Invoice status
- Available actions

## Searching Products

Users can search for products using the search controls provided at the top of the page.

Products can be searched by **Product ID** or using the general search field.

## Adding a Product

The **Add New** button allows users to create a new product in the system.

Users can enter the required product information and save the new product.

## Product Actions

The Actions column provides options for managing existing products.

Users can:

- Edit a product.
- Delete a product.

## Product Status

The **In Invoice?** field indicates whether the product is included in invoice processing.

## Pagination

The product list supports pagination, allowing users to navigate through multiple pages of products.

## Purpose

The Product List helps users maintain the products used by the billing system and manage their associated billing information.
