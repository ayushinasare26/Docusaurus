# Monthly Sales

The Monthly Sales section displays the sales performance of the business over a selected period.

![Monthly Sales](/img/monthly-sales.png)

*Figure: Monthly Sales chart showing customer sales across the year.*

## Filters

The Monthly Sales chart provides the following filters:

- **Month** — Select a specific month to view sales data.
- **Year** — Select a specific year to analyze sales performance.

These filters allow users to examine sales data for different time periods.

## Sales Chart

The chart provides a visual representation of monthly sales.

It allows users to:

- Identify sales trends.
- Compare performance across months.
- Monitor changes in sales.
- Understand overall business performance.

## Purpose

The Monthly Sales section helps users quickly understand how sales are performing over time and identify periods of higher or lower sales activity.
