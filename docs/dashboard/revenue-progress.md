# Revenue Progress

The Revenue Progress section provides an overview of the revenue generated by the business during the selected period.

![Revenue Progress](/img/revenue-progress.png)

*Figure: Revenue Progress showing monthly revenue, target, and progress.*

## Revenue Overview

The revenue progress indicator helps users understand the current revenue performance and compare it with the expected or targeted revenue.

## Purpose

The Revenue Progress section helps users:

- Monitor revenue performance.
- Track progress toward revenue targets.
- Identify whether revenue is increasing or decreasing.
- Get a quick overview of financial performance.

## How to Use

1. Open the **Dashboard**.
2. Locate the **Revenue Progress** section.
3. Review the displayed revenue value and progress indicator.
4. Use the information to evaluate the current revenue performance.
