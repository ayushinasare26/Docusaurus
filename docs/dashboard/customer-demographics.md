# Customer Demographics

The Customer Demographics section provides a geographical view of the customer base.

It uses a map-based visualization to show where customers are located.

## Purpose

The Customer Demographics section helps users:

- Understand the geographical distribution of customers.
- Identify areas with a higher concentration of customers.
- Analyze the customer base by location.
- Support geographical business planning.

## Map Visualization

Customer locations are displayed on an interactive map.

The map provides a visual representation of the geographical distribution of customers, making it easier to identify customer concentrations across different locations.

## Benefits

The demographic information can help the organization understand its customer distribution and identify potential opportunities in different geographical areas.
