# Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

The Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) section provides an estimate of the total value a customer is expected to generate over the entire duration of their relationship with the business.

![Customer Lifetime Value](/img/customer-lifetime-value.png)

*Figure: Customer Lifetime Value dashboard showing total revenue, average CLV, average monthly spending, and top customers.*

## Purpose

CLV helps the business understand the long-term value of its customers.

It can be used to:

- Identify high-value customers.
- Understand customer profitability.
- Support customer retention strategies.
- Make better business decisions.

## How to Use

1. Open the **Dashboard**.
2. Locate the **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** section.
3. Review the displayed customer value information.
4. Use the information to identify valuable customers and understand long-term customer performance.

## Benefits

Monitoring CLV helps the organization focus on customers who provide greater long-term value and supports informed customer management decisions.
