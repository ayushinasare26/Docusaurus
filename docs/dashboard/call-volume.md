# Call Volume Overview

The Call Volume Overview section displays information about the volume of calls handled by the business.

![Call Volume Overview](/img/call-volume.png)

*Figure: Call Volume Overview showing monthly call duration and call-volume trends.*

## Purpose

This section helps users understand call activity and monitor changes in call volume over time.

Users can use the call volume information to:

- Monitor overall call activity.
- Identify changes in call traffic.
- Understand customer communication patterns.
- Support operational planning.

## Call Volume Chart

The chart provides a visual representation of call activity, making it easier to identify periods with higher or lower call volumes.

Reviewing call volume alongside other dashboard metrics can help users understand overall business activity.
