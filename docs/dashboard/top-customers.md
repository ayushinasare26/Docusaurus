# Top Customers by Revenue

The Top Customers by Revenue section displays customers ranked according to the revenue they generate.

This allows users to quickly identify the customers contributing the most revenue to the business.

## Purpose

This section helps users:

- Identify high-revenue customers.
- Understand which customers contribute most to overall revenue.
- Prioritize important customer relationships.
- Support customer retention and business planning.

## Customer Ranking

Customers are displayed according to their revenue contribution.

The ranking provides a quick comparison between customers and helps identify the highest-value accounts.

## Benefits

Monitoring top customers by revenue helps the organization:

- Focus on high-value customers.
- Understand revenue concentration.
- Make informed customer management decisions.
- Develop strategies for customer retention and growth.
