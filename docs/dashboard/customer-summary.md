# Customer Summary Cards

The Customer Summary Cards are located at the top of the dashboard and provide an overview of the customer base.

![Customer Summary](/img/customer-summary.png)

*Figure: Customer Summary Cards displaying total, active, and suspended customers.*

## Total Customers

Displays the total number of customers registered in the system.

### Purpose

- Monitor business growth.
- Understand the overall customer base.
- Compare customer count over time.

### Example

**Total Customers: 605**

This indicates that there are 605 registered customers in the system.

## Active Customers

Displays customers whose accounts are currently active and whose services are operational.

### Purpose

- Track customers currently using services.
- Measure active subscriptions.
- Monitor customer engagement.

### Example

**Active Customers: 202**

This means 202 customers are actively using Hexa services.

## Suspended Customers

Displays customers whose services have been temporarily suspended due to reasons such as unpaid invoices, expired subscriptions, or administrative actions.

### Purpose

- Monitor suspended accounts.
- Identify customers requiring follow-up.
- Improve payment recovery.

### Example

**Suspended Customers: 403**

This indicates that 403 customer accounts are currently suspended.
