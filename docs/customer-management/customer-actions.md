# Customer Actions

The Customer Actions section provides operations that can be performed on customer records.

## Available Actions

Depending on the customer's status and available functionality, users can perform customer-related operations from the customer management interface.

These actions help users maintain and manage customer accounts efficiently.

## Purpose

Customer actions provide a centralized way to work with individual customer records without requiring users to navigate through multiple sections of the system.
