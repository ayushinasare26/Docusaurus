# Customer Management

The Customer Management section allows users to manage customer records and perform customer-related operations.

## Customer Management Features

The Customer Management section provides functionality for:

- Viewing customer records
- Searching for customers
- Filtering customer records
- Performing customer actions
- Adding new customers

## Customer List

The customer list provides an overview of customers stored in the system.

Users can review customer information and access individual customer records.

## Purpose

Customer Management helps users maintain accurate customer information and efficiently manage customer accounts.
