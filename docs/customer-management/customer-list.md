# Customer List

The Customer List displays the customers available in the Hexa Billing System.

The list provides users with a centralized location to view and manage customer records.

![Customer List](/img/customer-list.png)

*Figure: Customer List displaying customer records, status, search, filtering, and available actions.*

## Information

Customer records can be reviewed from the customer list to help users manage the customer base efficiently.

## Actions

Users can select a customer record to access additional customer-related information and available actions.

## Purpose

The Customer List helps users:

- View customer records.
- Access customer information.
- Manage customer accounts.
- Quickly navigate through the customer database.
