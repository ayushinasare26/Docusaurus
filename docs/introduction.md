# Hexa Billing System

## About Hexa Billing System

The Hexa Billing System is a complete web-based billing and customer management platform designed specifically for VoIP and telecom businesses.

It enables organizations to efficiently manage:

- Customers
- Services
- Billing
- Invoices
- Payments
- Telecom resources
- Business reports

The system provides a centralized dashboard for managing these operations.

Instead of manually maintaining spreadsheets or paper invoices, the system automates billing operations, reduces errors, and provides real-time insights into business performance.

Whether you are an administrator, accountant, sales representative, or support executive, the Hexa Billing System helps streamline daily operations while ensuring accurate financial records.

## Objectives

The system is designed to:

- Simplify customer management
- Automate invoice generation
- Track customer payments
- Manage telecom resources such as DIDs
- Maintain Call Detail Records (CDRs)
- Generate detailed business reports
- Improve operational efficiency
